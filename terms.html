Premium subscriptions

Premium is sold only as auto-renewable subscriptions: Premium Weekly, which renews every week, and Premium Yearly, which renews every year (now and then offered at a lower yearly price). The app shows the exact price and period before you buy. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.

A subscription renews automatically at the same price for the same period unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or in the app’s Settings › Manage subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for. A family member can share your subscription through Family Sharing.

Community rules

In Chirplet you can add friends and raise a pet together with one other person. They see the names you choose (your name, your pets’ names and the names of your Pet Studio forms) and your Pet Studio drawings. The first time you add a friend, accept one, invite someone to raise a pet with you or join a shared pet, the app shows these rules and asks you to agree to them. They are part of these terms, alongside the license agreement above.

  • There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Do not use names or drawings that are hateful, sexual, violent, harassing or otherwise offensive, and do not bully, threaten or impersonate anyone.
  • Report anyone who breaks them: on the Friends page, open the menu next to their name and choose Report; for a pet you raise together, use Report in the app’s Settings. You can also write to yasinertekinn@outlook.com.
  • We review reports within 24 hours. When names or drawings break these rules, we hide them for everyone and remove the person who sent them from friends and sharing in Chirplet: their friendships end, their invitations and friend requests are refused, and they no longer raise a pet with anyone.
  • You can block a friend, or someone who asked to be friends, from the same menu at any time. A pet you raise together can be left, or no longer shared, in Settings.
