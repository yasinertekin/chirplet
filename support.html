Support

Write to us at yasinertekinn@outlook.com. If you send feedback from the app’s Settings, it opens an email to this address with your app version, iOS version and device model added.

How do I get my pets back on a new iPhone?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on the new iPhone, make sure iCloud is on for the app, and open the app. Your pets are saved in your own iCloud account and come back from there.

How do I raise a pet together with someone?

You invite one person with a link, and only that person can join. The pet then lives in the owner’s iCloud and is shared with the other person. Either of you can stop at any time: the owner keeps the pet, and the other person keeps a card of it on their own iPhone.

How do friends and invite links work?

Someone becomes your friend only when you send them a link and say yes to who joined. If your friend does not have the app yet, the link can open the App Clip, which shows your pet. The App Clip sends nothing to the developer.

How do I report or block someone?

On the Friends page, open the menu next to a friend’s name, or next to someone who asked to be friends, and choose Report or Block. For a pet you raise together, Report is in the app’s Settings, where you can also leave the pet or stop sharing it. We review reports within 24 hours; when names or drawings break the rules, we hide them for everyone and remove the person who sent them from friends and sharing. The community rules say what is not allowed.

How do I restore Premium?

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases. The app reads your purchase from the App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with. Please do not buy it again.

What does the Apple Watch app do?

The Watch app keeps its own copy of your pets and syncs with your iCloud account, the same way the iPhone does. iCloud has to be on for it to find your pets. The Watch app and its complications are part of Premium, which you get in the iPhone app; nothing is sold on the Watch.

Where are the stickers?

Open Messages, start a message, and look for the app’s stickers in the Messages app drawer. Stickers are made from your pets on your iPhone.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app deletes what it keeps on this iPhone. iOS may keep one item after that: the random number the app made for this iPhone for the relay, in the iPhone’s keychain, which says nothing about you. To delete the copy in iCloud too, go to Settings, tap your name, then iCloud and the storage list, and delete the app’s data. What you added to a pet or a friendship someone else started stays in their iCloud until they remove it. To have what Adapty, Google and Meta received, or any feedback or report email we hold from or about you (except a report that keeps someone on the moderation list; a person who was removed may ask us to review it), deleted, write to us at the address above. The details are in the privacy policy.
